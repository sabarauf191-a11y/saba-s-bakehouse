About Saba's Bakehouse

Hi there 👋 I'm Saba! Baking is my happy place — I genuinely love every step, from mixing the batter to watching loaves rise in the oven, to carefully packing each order.

For me, it's not just about desserts; it's about the smile that a box of baked goods can bring and the warmth that homemade treats add to someone's day.

I specialize in cookies and loaves, with a soft spot for cozy & traditional style inspired flavors. All bakes are made fresh on a pre-order basis to keep that homemade taste and quality in every bite.

Saba's Bakehouse is proud to serve our local community with treats that feel like a warm hug.

Baked with love, served with care 🧸

How to Place an Order

Placing an order is simple and cozy — just like our bakes 💌

You can:

  • 📱 Message me on Instagram: @saba_bakehouse
  • 📧 Email: sababakehouse@gmail.com

I also take catering and bulk orders with a minimum 2 weeks' notice so there's enough time to plan, prep, and bake everything fresh.

For each order, please share:

  • What you'd like to order (cookies, loaves, or custom box)
  • Quantity
  • Preferred pickup/delivery date
  • Any flavor preferences or notes

I'll get back to you with availability, pricing details, and next steps. 🤎
